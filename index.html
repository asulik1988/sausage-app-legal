Important Safety Notices

  • Proper ingredient sourcing and quality
  • Correct handling and sanitation

2. Your Responsibility

As the user, YOU are solely responsible for:

  • Verifying all recipes and calculations
  • Following proper food safety guidelines
  • Understanding and using curing salts correctly
  • Maintaining proper temperatures during preparation
  • Ensuring ingredients are fresh and safe
  • Consulting professionals when uncertain

3. Specific Hazards

🧂 Curing Salt Warning

  • Prague Powder #1 and #2 contain sodium nitrite/nitrate
  • Incorrect use can cause serious illness or death
  • Keep away from children
  • Never exceed recommended amounts (0.25% typically)
  • Do not confuse with regular salt

🌡️ Temperature Dangers

  • Ground meat must reach 160°F (71°C) for safety
  • Smoking requires precise temperature control
  • Cold smoking without proper knowledge is dangerous
  • Improper temperatures can cause botulism or other serious illness

5. Professional Consultation

  • Taking certified food safety courses

  • Using professional recipes for cured meats
  • Getting proper equipment and thermometers

6. High-Risk Individuals
